How to use:
1. My Profile — save your name, addresses, phones, emails so scans and opt-outs use your real data.
2. Data Brokers — click "Automate" to let Playwright fill opt-out forms, or open the opt-out link manually. Track status.
3. Documents — upload a file or paste text; get a redacted TXT + PDF.
4. CCPA Templates — generate pre-filled deletion/opt-out emails for any broker.
5. Settings — configure email reminders so you're notified when it's time to re-check.
